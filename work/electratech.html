Context

Hackathon: Tech in Real Life

At DUT Hackathon 2023, our team set out to reduce household energy waste with a smart power outlet that recommends and automates energy-saving behaviour.

Problem

Energy waste from habits nobody tracks

  • Households waste electricity through standby devices and unmonitored usage patterns.
  • Existing smart plugs switch devices on and off but do not learn from behaviour or recommend savings.
  • We had to define real market needs and product behaviour before building anything.
My role

Owner of the product definition and integration

  • Researched the market need and defined product behaviour.
  • Handled analysis, prototyping and integration between the IoT outlet, the AI recommendation logic and the app.
  • Contributed to the prototype demo and documentation for the judges.
Process

Hackathon-speed product discovery

STEP 01

Research

Market needs and user pain points; competitor smart-plug behaviour.

STEP 02

Define

Product behaviour: what the outlet measures, learns, recommends and automates.

STEP 03

Prototype

IoT outlet + behaviour-based AI energy optimisation + app screens (usage, money, providers).

STEP 04

Integrate

Connected hardware, recommendation logic and app into one demo flow.

STEP 05

Pitch

Demo and documentation for the hackathon jury.

Learnings

Takeaways

  • Defining product behaviour first made a five-person team move like a much larger one.
  • Integration is where hackathon projects die — planning it from day one is what made the demo work.
  • A clear story of user need → behaviour → benefit is as important as the code.
